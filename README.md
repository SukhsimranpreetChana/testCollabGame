# NO ANSWER

A first-person psychological horror game built in Unity.

Inspired by looping hallway horror experiences, PT, traps the player inside a seemingly ordinary hallway that becomes increasingly scary with every loop.

The environment changes, the broadcasts change, the phone keeps ringing, and something is getting closer.

## About the Game

No Answer is a short atmospheric psychological horror experience focused on environmental storytelling and a continuously changing hallway to scare the player (hopefully lol).

## Gameplay

The player explores a repeating hallway and interacts with objects throughout the environment.

Each completed hallway loop advances the game to the next stage.

Some loops require the player to:

- Listen to television broadcasts
- Answer the telephone
- Investigate environmental changes
- Approach specific areas
- Survive encounters
- Escape the monster

Progress is primarily driven through exploration and interaction.

## Loop System

The game is structured around seven hallway loops.

### Loop 1

The hallway appears relatively normal.

The player is introduced to the environment and basic controls.

### Loop 2

A television broadcast begins reporting an incident.

The player must listen to the report before the telephone begins ringing.

### Loop 3

The broadcasts become increasingly unusual.

A monster appears in the hallway.

### Loop 4

The hallway becomes quiet.

A toy car moves through the hallway while playing music.

### Loop 5

The television broadcast becomes distorted and unstable.

The environment continues to deteriorate.

### Loop 6

A monster encounter triggers a chase sequence through the hallway.

### Loop 7

The final loop.

The environment and story reach their conclusion.
